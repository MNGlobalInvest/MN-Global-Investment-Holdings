### Company Overview & Core Thesis

> **Core Thesis:** Turn one core skill (fixing business workflows) into high-margin services, digital products, owned traffic, and proprietary automation—owning every layer of the digital stack through Digital Vertical Integration—then compound operating profits with discipline.

Founded on **May 21, 2013**, **MN Global Investment Holdings, LLC** operates as an AI-first, remote-first holding company with zero W-2 employee payroll. Self-hosted software, open-source AI models, private Ubuntu cloud servers, and automated pipelines execute repeat operations, while vetted independent contractors handle physical tasks. The founder retains direct ownership over corporate strategy, capital allocation, infrastructure security, `RISK` exception review, and final quality verification.

#### Operating Principles & Digital Vertical Integration Doctrine

- **Systems Beat Effort:** If a task happens twice, an automated system is built for it. Any process repeated more than three times is codified into a written Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) with a named owner and measured cycle time.
- **Zero Payroll Overhead:** Operations scale through self-hosted software, sovereign AI pipelines, and vetted independent contractors rather than W-2 headcount.
- **Cash Is Oxygen:** Operating profit and cash velocity precede expansion spending; initiatives are funded strictly from retained operating cash flow and recovered capital without debt.
- **Own the Digital Bottleneck (Digital Vertical Integration):** Control the scarcest layers of the stack—private VPS compute, local open-source LLM inference, deterministic OCR extraction pipelines, owned email distribution, and internal databases—rather than relying on fragile third-party SaaS wrappers.
- **Founder Reviews Exceptions:** Automation executes the first operational pass; the founder reviews `RISK` flags and strategic decisions.
- **Strict Separation:** Company capital, infrastructure, and accounting are maintained in complete separation from personal accounts.

### Operational Workflows & Governance

<details>
<summary><strong>End-to-End Execution Flows (Click to Expand)</strong></summary>
<br />

1. **Audit Client Execution Flow**
   - Client books and pays upfront via the storefront and submits operational/financial exports to `info@mnglobalcompliance.com`.
   - A Python ingestion daemon validates, hashes, and logs each file on MN Global's private Ubuntu VPS.
   - `Tesseract` / `PaddleOCR` and `pdfplumber` pre-process raw files before a local quantized `Qwen` model extracts structured JSON records.
   - A deterministic rules engine evaluates cash conversion velocity, billing leakage, and manual bottlenecks, tagging every finding as `SAFE` or `RISK`.
   - The founder manually reviews all `RISK` exceptions and approves the final executive report alongside a tailored Implementation and Monthly Care Plan proposal.

2. **Customer Acquisition & Digital Product Funnel**
   - Prospective operators discover standardized educational pins via organic Pinterest search (`Traffic`) and download the free **Home-Services Self-Audit Checklist** (`Subscribe`).
   - Automated email sequences deliver actionable operational frameworks and introduce the `$29`, `$49`, and `$79` self-serve products (`Buy`).
   - Post-purchase sequences invite buyers to apply their product investment toward a fixed-fee **Workflow Audit** (`Audit`), followed by turnkey deployment (`Implement`) and recurring **Monthly Care Plans** (`Retain`).

3. **Data Sovereignty & Security Governance**
   - Client documents never leave MN Global's private server infrastructure.
   - Open-source model licenses are verified for commercial compliance prior to installation and audited quarterly.
   - Independent contractors operate under strict least-privilege controls with zero access to company bank accounts, storefront administration, or VPS root credentials.
