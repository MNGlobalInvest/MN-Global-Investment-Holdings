### 🏛️ About & Core Thesis

> **Core Thesis:** Turn one core skill (fixing business workflows) into high-margin services, digital products, owned traffic, and proprietary automation—owning every layer of the digital stack through Digital Vertical Integration—then compound operating profits with discipline.

Founded by **Terry Williams** on **May 21, 2013**, **MN Global Investment Holdings, LLC** is a Minnesota Single-Member LLC in good standing operating as an AI-first, remote-first company with zero employee payroll. Software, self-hosted AI, cloud servers, and automated workflows execute repeat operations, while vetted independent contractors handle physical tasks.

- 🔭 **Currently Building:** Deterministic document ingestion pipelines, local AI workflow audits, and self-serve operational playbooks for small home-service and local service businesses.
- 🧠 **Operating Doctrine:** *Systems beat effort.* Automation handles the first pass while the founder owns strategy, capital allocation, security, `RISK` exceptions, and final quality verification.
- 🛡️ **Data Sovereignty:** Client documents remain on MN Global's private Ubuntu VPS—zero external commercial AI API calls with client data.
- 📍 **Footprint:** Remote-first with general business correspondence headquartered in Memphis, Tennessee.

- **Audit Client Flow:** Client books and pays upfront on the storefront &rarr; sends operational/financial files to `info@mnglobalcompliance.com` &rarr; Python ingestion daemon validates, hashes, and logs files &rarr; OCR (`Tesseract`/`PaddleOCR` + `pdfplumber`) and local `Qwen` extract structured JSON &rarr; deterministic rules engine tags findings `SAFE` or `RISK` &rarr; founder reviews `RISK` exceptions and approves the executive report with Implementation & Care Plan options.
- **Digital Product Customer Flow:** Visitor discovers a Pinterest pin and downloads the free **Home-Services Self-Audit Checklist** &rarr; automated email sequence shares practical tips and introduces the `$29`, `$49`, and `$79` products &rarr; instant digital delivery and buyer tagging &rarr; post-purchase sequence offers credit toward a paid Workflow Audit.
